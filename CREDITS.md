# Asset and service credits

- Company names, logos, favicons and trademarks belong to their respective owners. They are used solely to identify the company selected by the user; their presence does not imply affiliation or endorsement.
- The 1,140 original company marks are stored locally in `assets/logos/`; they were retrieved from companies' public website domains through Icon Horse (https://icon.horse/) and Uplead's public logo endpoint (https://logo.uplead.com/). Groupon's local SVG is from theSVG (CC0-1.0; https://thesvg.org/icons/groupon/default.svg), and Debenhams' 2018 wordmark is from Wikimedia Commons (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Debenhams_logo_2018.svg). Company marks remain the property of their respective owners.
- For companies outside the local directory, the server verifies company identity and official domains with Wikidata, then checks the official site for a logo or favicon and Wikidata Commons for a company logo. Verified image bytes and their source URLs are cached in Supabase; a company is still selectable by name if no verified logo is available. Wikimedia Commons files may carry individual licenses; the cache records each logo's source URL and source type.
- Case delivery uses Web3Forms (https://web3forms.com/). The site has no analytics, advertising trackers, external font requests or CSS framework runtime. Company discovery and logo retrieval make server-side requests to Wikidata and verified official company domains.
- GetItResolved branding and the social preview artwork were created for this project.
