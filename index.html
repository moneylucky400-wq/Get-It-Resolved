Before you submit

Clear information about references, company affiliation, regional coverage and locally saved cases.

How do I get a GetItResolved case reference?

Select a company, complete the required contact and issue details, accept the submission notice, and submit the form. After successful delivery, GetItResolved displays a unique GIR case reference and saves the case in your current browser.

Is GetItResolved affiliated with the companies listed?

No. GetItResolved is an independent documentation portal and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official support channel for the listed companies. Company names and logos are used only to identify the organisation concerned.

Can I track a case on another device?

The tracker reads cases saved in your current browser. It does not synchronise across devices. Clearing browser storage can remove the locally saved record, so keep a separate copy of your reference and submission details.

Which companies and regions does the directory cover?

The directory includes telecoms, airlines, energy providers, banks, retailers, couriers and subscription services across the United Kingdom, United States, Europe, Canada, Australia and global markets. You can also search for any other company by name and submit a case against it.

Customer complaint support across major service sectors

Use GetItResolved for billing and direct-debit errors, refund requests, missing deliveries, service outages, cancellation and rebooking requests, parking-charge appeals and other unresolved customer service issues. The directory covers telecom and broadband providers, energy and utility companies, airlines and travel platforms, banks and fintech services, retailers, couriers and subscription businesses.
